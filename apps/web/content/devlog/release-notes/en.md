A post that belongs to no folder sits at the top level of its section. In the sidebar it appears below the folders; on the index page it appears after the grouped sections.

## 2026.08

- Added the hierarchical outline on the left. Folders collapse and remember their last state.
- Linked posts to each other with previous/next in outline order.
- Detail pages now show the folder path of the current post.

## 2026.07

- Moved the content directory to a nested structure.
- Locked static rendering in as the default.
