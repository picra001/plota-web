---
title: "Why a read-only site, on purpose"
description: "Writing lives elsewhere. Here is only reading — light, fast, and good for search."
---

This site has no writing screen. That is the point. Editing happens in other tools; only finished posts arrive here.

So a page is little more than text and images. It loads fast, folds cleanly on mobile, and reads well for search engines.

Trust comes from consistency, not flourish. One post a week, in five languages. That is all this domain is quietly building.
